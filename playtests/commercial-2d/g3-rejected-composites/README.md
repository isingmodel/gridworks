# Rejected G.3 composite candidates

These 23 PNGs and their former Godot import records are recoverable visual-production history only. They
were moved out of `game/art` on 2026-08-22 because each is unbound, superseded, or owns more than one
composition unit. They are not runtime resources and must never be copied back into a scene or package.

The set includes every former district, parcel, cluster, neighborhood, hamlet, compound yard/corridor and
the unprovenanced river-bank rubble candidate. Exact prompts and generated-source hashes remain in
`game/art/commercial/g3-assets.prompts.md` and `playtests/commercial-2d/g3-runtime-sources/`.

Step 1's accepted city is assembled only from the 12 files under `game/art/commercial/g3/atomic/`, the six
road files under `game/art/commercial/g3/roads/`, and explicit renderer placement records.
